US Bombs Iran After Hormuz Tanker Attacks

BREAKING: The US has launched military strikes on Iran following attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. This is not a drill — active conflict between the US and Iran. 🧵

What triggered it: Tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz — the narrow waterway that carries roughly 20% of the world's oil supply. The US blamed Iran and responded with strikes, saying it intends to impose 'heavy costs.'

Iran's response: Tehran is warning it will 'take decisive measures.' That language is deliberately vague — it could mean proxy attacks, naval escalation, or missile strikes. Nobody knows yet how far they're willing to go.

Why this hits everyone: The Strait of Hormuz is the world's single most important oil chokepoint. Any serious disruption there sends energy prices spiking globally — at the pump, in supply chains, everywhere.

The core question now: Does this stay contained as a sharp exchange, or does Iran's retaliation trigger another US response? That loop is how regional conflicts become something much larger. Watch this one closely.
