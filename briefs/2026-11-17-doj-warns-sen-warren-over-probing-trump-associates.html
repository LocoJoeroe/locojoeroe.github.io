DOJ Warns Sen. Warren Over Probing Trump Associates

BREAKING: A sitting U.S. attorney formally warned Sen. Elizabeth Warren of 'legal consequences' if she continues investigating Trump associates. That's not a legal threat — it's a signal to all of Congress.

The warning told Warren she must preserve records related to Trump investigations or face unspecified consequences. It's the DOJ — an executive branch agency — telling a senator her oversight work has a price.

Here's what makes this significant: Congress has a constitutional role in oversight. The executive branch warning a legislator off that work is a direct shot at the separation of powers, not just one senator.

Legal experts note the DOJ's actual leverage over a sitting senator is limited. But the chilling effect doesn't require a conviction — it just requires the threat to land. And it has.
