Ukraine Just Launched One of Its Biggest Drone Attacks Ever

Ukraine launched hundreds of drones across Russia on Sunday in one of the biggest aerial assaults of the war, according to NPR.

At least 6 people were killed inside Russian territory. The scale puts this among Kyiv's most ambitious strikes since the war began.

This isn't a defensive move — Ukraine is deliberately hitting deep inside Russia, forcing Moscow to defend its own skies instead of just pressing forward in the east.

The shift matters: drone campaigns this large strain Russian air defenses, disrupt logistics, and hit civilian confidence in ways frontline battles don't. Kyiv is opening a second front — from above.
