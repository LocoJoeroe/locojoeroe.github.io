White House Threatens Democrats: Oversight Could Be a Crime

BREAKING: The White House sent a formal warning to Democrats saying aggressive investigations of Trump officials could constitute a crime. That's not spin — that's a written threat to Congress's core constitutional role.

Congressional oversight isn't a partisan perk. It's how the legislative branch checks the executive. The power to investigate — subpoena, compel testimony, demand documents — has existed since the First Congress in 1789.

The White House framing: pursue us too hard and YOU could be prosecuted. Democrats' response, per the top Oversight member: 'We're not bending the knee.'

Here's why this is alarming regardless of party: if the executive branch can criminalize oversight, it can effectively make itself uninvestigable. That's not a Trump-Democrat fight. That's a structural threat to how American government is supposed to work.

The NYT headline called it threatening Congress. The New Republic said the White House 'has no clue how Congress works.' CNN called it hypocrisy. Rare editorial alignment across outlets — the substance here is real.
