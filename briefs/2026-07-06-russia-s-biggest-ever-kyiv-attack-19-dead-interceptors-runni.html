Russia's Biggest Ever Kyiv Attack: 19 Dead, Interceptors Running Out

Russia just launched its biggest single attack on Kyiv: 68 missiles, 351 drones, 19 people killed. Zelensky is calling it a 'massive attack' — and he's warning Ukraine is running out of interceptors to stop the next one.

The scale is staggering. 351 strike drones alone is more than most countries launch in a month of operations. Ukraine's air defenses absorbed the hits but couldn't stop everything — and Zelensky says the stockpiles are draining fast.

Timing matters: this attack landed days before a major NATO summit where air defense resupply was already the central agenda item. Russia just made the argument for Ukraine louder than any diplomat could.

The core problem: interceptor missiles cost far more than the drones they're shooting down, and Ukraine is burning through them faster than allies are restocking them. That math eventually fails.

Watch the NATO summit closely now. What member states commit — or don't — in the next 72 hours is a direct response to 19 dead in Kyiv. Follow for breaking news as this develops.
