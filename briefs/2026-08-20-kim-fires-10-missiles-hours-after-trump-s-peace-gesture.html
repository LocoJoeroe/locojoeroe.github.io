Kim Fires 10 Missiles Hours After Trump's Peace Gesture

Trump ordered the US to scale back joint military drills with South Korea — an apparent olive branch to Kim Jong-un. Kim's answer came the very next day.

North Korea launched roughly 10 short-range ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang region, detected by both South Korea and Japan. All landed at sea.

This is the oldest move in Kim's playbook: pocket the concession, then launch anyway. The drills were meant to signal goodwill. The missiles signal something else.

The problem with unilateral gestures toward Pyongyang is that they cost the US (and South Korea) real deterrence, while Kim pays nothing and gives nothing. One day. Ten missiles.

The question now is whether Trump treats this as a setback to his diplomatic strategy — or doubles down anyway. Either answer matters enormously for South Korea and Japan.
