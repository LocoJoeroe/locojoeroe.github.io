The Daughter of a Jailed Dictator Just Became Peru's President

Keiko Fujimori — daughter of jailed former dictator Alberto Fujimori — just became Peru's president. She won narrowly and immediately promised an 'iron fist' against crime. Sound familiar?

At 51, Keiko has run for president three times and lost twice. She's also faced her own corruption charges. Peru's politics are brutal, and she knows it — she's survived it for decades.

Her father Alberto ruled Peru through the 1990s with authoritarian tactics: death squads, mass sterilizations, widespread corruption. He died in prison. She says she's different.

The uncomfortable truth: Peru's crime crisis is real, and voters chose her anyway — knowingly. 'Iron fist' isn't just rhetoric; it's what a desperate electorate asked for.

The question Latin America is watching: does 'strong on crime' stop at actual crime, or does it eventually reach political opponents and the press? Peru has seen that movie before. #Peru
