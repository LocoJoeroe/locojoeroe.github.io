60,000 Crossed Into Spain in One Day — 41 Didn't Make It

BREAKING: Spain just experienced the largest single-day border crossing in European history — and 41 people are dead.

Up to 50,000-60,000 migrants crossed from Morocco into Ceuta, a tiny Spanish enclave on the African coast, in a matter of hours. Many swam across. Dozens didn't survive.

Spanish PM Pedro Sánchez called it a "violation of territorial integrity." Other EU nations are pointing fingers at Madrid, asking how this was allowed to happen at all.

Morocco effectively opened the border — widely seen as leverage in a separate diplomatic dispute with Spain. It's a pressure tactic dressed up as a migration surge.

The EU's external border is only as strong as the bilateral deals holding it together. Today, one of those deals collapsed in real time. This won't be the last time.
