US Strikes Iranian Launchers on Larak Island — First Hit in Weeks

BREAKING: The US struck Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island — the first known US attack on Iran in over a month.

Tehran says the strike killed civilians. Larak Island sits at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz — one of the most critical shipping chokepoints on the planet.

This isn't a random target. Launchers on Larak can threaten the tanker traffic that moves roughly 20% of the world's seaborne oil.

The pause since late July didn't mean things were cooling down. It meant both sides were repositioning. Tonight's strike suggests the US is keeping pressure on Iran's ability to threaten the strait — not winding down.

The real question: does hitting the launchers deter Iran from replacing them, or does it just accelerate the next escalation? Hormuz tensions don't resolve — they cycle.
