Iran Leaves Doha as a Draft U.S.-Iran Proposal Circulates

BREAKING: Iranian negotiators have physically left Doha — but a draft proposal for a U.S.-Iran deal is now circulating, per Reuters. The talks didn't end. They just got complicated.

Iran's position: Hormuz security gets 'restored' if the U.S. agrees to their terms. That's the Strait through which ~20% of global oil flows. Tehran knows exactly what lever it's holding.

Iran says 'the ball is in the U.S. court.' They've reportedly proposed a 2-day plan: reopen the Strait, resume nuclear talks. Whether Washington bites is the entire question right now.

Walking out while leaving a draft on the table is a classic pressure move — force the other side to respond publicly or look like they killed the deal. Iran just made the U.S. the villain in the next headline, whatever happens.
