Saudi Arabia Shuts Main Oil Pipeline After Drone Strikes From Iraq

BREAKING: Saudi Arabia has shut down its East-West crude oil pipeline after multiple drone strikes launched from Iraq. This is not a minor hit — this is the artery.

The East-West pipeline is specifically designed to bypass the Strait of Hormuz — the world's most critical oil chokepoint. If that bypass route goes down, there is no alternative path for Gulf crude to reach global markets.

Oil was already trading above $100 a barrel before this attack. A sustained shutdown of this pipeline doesn't just move prices — it removes the safety valve that was keeping a Hormuz crisis from becoming catastrophic.

Iraq's prime minister responded by dismissing a military commander following the attack — a signal Baghdad knows this crossed a line. But the drones came anyway, pointing to Iran-aligned militia groups acting outside official Iraqi control.
