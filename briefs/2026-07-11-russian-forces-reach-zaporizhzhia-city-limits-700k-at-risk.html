Russian Forces Reach Zaporizhzhia City Limits — 700K at Risk

BREAKING: Russian forces have reached the outskirts of Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine's sixth-largest city. The mayor confirmed it. Emergency crews are searching rubble after an air strike hit the city.

This is a significant escalation. Zaporizhzhia has 700,000 people — and Europe's largest nuclear power plant sits just downstream. Urban combat near either is a serious crisis.

Ukraine's military has held this city since the start of the full-scale invasion. Russia controlling the plant's region but not the city has been an uneasy status quo for two years.

If street fighting reaches a city this size, civilian casualties scale fast. And any military activity near the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant raises the stakes for all of Europe — not just Ukraine.

This is the front line moving somewhere it hasn't moved before. Watch this one closely.
