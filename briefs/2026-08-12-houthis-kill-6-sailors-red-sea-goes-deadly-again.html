Houthis Kill 6 Sailors — Red Sea Goes Deadly Again

BREAKING: The Red Sea has its first sailor deaths in over 14 months. Houthis hit cargo vessels, killing 6 crew. The U.S. struck back against a container ship. The low-simmer phase of this conflict just ended.

For most of the past year, Houthi attacks had disrupted shipping without killing crew. That changed. Six sailors are dead after strikes on a cargo ship and a container ship in the Red Sea and Gulf of Oman.

The U.S. responded by striking a container ship — CNBC reports two concrete military actions in rapid sequence. This isn't a warning shot situation; both sides moved fast.

Why it matters beyond the headlines: the Red Sea handles roughly 12% of global trade. Every escalation there ripples into shipping costs, insurance rates, and supply chains that are already fragile.

The question now is whether this stays a tit-for-tat exchange or becomes a sustained hot phase. Fourteen months of 'no fatalities' created a false floor. That floor is gone.
