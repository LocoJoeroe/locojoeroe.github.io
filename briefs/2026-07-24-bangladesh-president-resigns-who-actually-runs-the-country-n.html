Bangladesh President Resigns — Who Actually Runs the Country Now?

BREAKING: Bangladesh President Mohammed Shahabuddin has resigned — two years before his term was up. Official reason: health. Real context: this country is still in political freefall.

Shahabuddin, 76, was president during last year's mass uprising that forced Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina to flee the country. He was never a strong executive — Bangladesh's president is largely ceremonial — but his exit still matters.

Why? Because Bangladesh is already being governed by an interim administration with no democratic mandate. Losing the head of state too leaves a serious legitimacy vacuum at the top of a nation of 170 million people.

"Health reasons" in a politically volatile country is a phrase that deserves a raised eyebrow. Whether this was pressure, exhaustion, or genuinely medical — the timing is striking either way.

Bangladesh has been one of the most important and underreported political stories of the past year. This is not the end of that story. Follow for breaking news.
