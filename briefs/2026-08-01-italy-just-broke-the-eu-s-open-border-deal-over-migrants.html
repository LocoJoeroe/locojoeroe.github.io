Italy Just Broke the EU's Open Border Deal Over Migrants

BREAKING: Italy has suspended the Schengen Agreement with Spain — closing air and sea borders between the two countries. This is a major rupture inside the EU. Here's why it happened. 🧵

It started in Ceuta — a tiny Spanish territory on the African coast, right on the Moroccan border. Around 60,000 migrants crossed in a short window, overwhelming the enclave and sending shockwaves through European capitals.

Spain's PM is blaming traffickers. But Italy isn't waiting for explanations — it's slamming the door, arguing migrants are moving through Spain into Italy across Schengen's invisible borders.

Schengen is the deal that lets 400 million Europeans travel freely without passport checks. Italy suspending it with a fellow member is one of the most dramatic unilateral moves we've seen inside the bloc in years.
