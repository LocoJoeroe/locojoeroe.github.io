39 Dead, Hundreds of Cobras Loose in China Flooding

Southern China is dealing with one of the uglier natural disasters in recent memory. 39 dead, a tornado that blew a man out of his 12th-floor apartment, and floodwaters still rising. That's the baseline.

A typhoon triggered the initial flooding. Then came rare inland tornadoes. Then the snakes. China has allocated emergency disaster relief funds, but the situation on the ground is still active and dangerous.
