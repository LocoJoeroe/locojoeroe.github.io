Warren Buffett Steps Down After More Than 50 Years at Berkshire Hathaway

BREAKING: Warren Buffett is stepping down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway after more than 50 years. His son Howard will take the role.

To put this in scale: Buffett took control of Berkshire in 1965. Since then the stock has compounded at roughly 20% annually — one of the greatest wealth-creation runs in financial history.

Howard Buffett is not Warren Buffett. He's a philanthropist and farmer, not an investor. His job is to protect the culture his father built — not to replicate the genius.

The real question isn't whether Berkshire collapses. It won't overnight. The question is whether any institution can survive the loss of the one person who *was* the institution.

If you're one of the millions of investors whose retirement is tied to Berkshire, now is the time to understand what you actually own — and what you owned was partly just Warren Buffett's judgment.
