US Just Gave Saudi Arabia a Path to Enrich Uranium

BREAKING: The US has reached a deal that could allow Saudi Arabia to enrich uranium for a civilian nuclear program. That's not a small thing. 🧵

Uranium enrichment is the same process used to make nuclear fuel — and, at higher levels, nuclear weapons. The Saudis have long said they want civilian power. Critics don't fully buy it.

Saudi Crown Prince MBS has said openly: if Iran gets a nuclear weapon, Saudi Arabia will pursue one too. This deal doesn't hand them a bomb — but it hands them a step closer to the capability.

Israel is watching closely. Iran hawks in Washington are furious. The traditional U.S. nonproliferation playbook — the '123 Agreement' — typically requires countries to give up enrichment rights. Riyadh apparently got a carve-out.

Bottom line: Trump approved this during active Iran nuclear negotiations. Whether that's leverage or a contradiction depends entirely on what happens next.
