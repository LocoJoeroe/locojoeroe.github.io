Supreme Court Clears Trump to Deport Migrants to Third Countries

The Supreme Court just handed Trump a major immigration win: migrants can now be deported to third countries — places they're not from and may never have been to.

The Court's ruling is in effect immediately, even as the legal fight continues. Lower courts had blocked the policy — SCOTUS overruled them, at least for now.

What does 'third country' mean? If someone is from, say, Venezuela but crossed through Panama, the administration can potentially send them to a country other than their home — including ones they have no connection to.

The big question hanging over this: what legal protections do migrants get before being sent somewhere they didn't come from? The Court is set to hear full arguments on that soon.

Bottom line: this is one of the most sweeping immigration enforcement tools the Trump admin has sought. SCOTUS just said use it — for now.
