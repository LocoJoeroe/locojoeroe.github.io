Trump: No Nuclear Deal for Saudis Without Recognizing Israel

BREAKING: Trump says Saudi Arabia must recognize Israel to get a U.S. nuclear energy deal. He's tying the Abraham Accords directly to nuclear cooperation. This is a massive ultimatum.

The deal would let Saudi Arabia develop civilian nuclear energy with U.S. help — something Riyadh has wanted for years. But Trump is now making it conditional on joining the Abraham Accords, the normalization agreements Israel signed with UAE, Bahrain, and others.

Saudi Arabia has long said it won't normalize with Israel without a credible path to Palestinian statehood. That position hasn't changed. So Trump just put two immovable objects on a collision course.

If the Saudis walk away, the deal collapses and the U.S. loses major leverage in the Gulf. If they accept, it reshapes the entire Middle East order — and infuriates much of the Arab world. There's no easy middle ground here.

This isn't a throwaway comment. Trump publicly conditioning a nuclear deal on Israel recognition is a live geopolitical grenade. Watch this one closely.
